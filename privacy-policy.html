Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Galaxy Digital Advertisers handles information when you visit our website or contact us about our services.

Last Updated: 6 November 2026

1. Introduction

Galaxy Digital Advertisers ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and aims to handle personal information responsibly. This policy describes the types of information that may be collected through our website and how that information may be used.

2. Information We May Collect

When you contact us or interact with our website, we may receive information such as:

  • Your name and business name.
  • Your email address and contact details.
  • Your website URL or publishing business information.
  • Information you voluntarily include in an inquiry.
  • Basic technical information, such as browser type and device information, where applicable.

3. How We Use Information

Information provided to us may be used to:

  • Respond to business inquiries and requests.
  • Discuss publisher monetization opportunities.
  • Evaluate potential business and referral partnerships.
  • Provide information about our services.
  • Maintain, secure, and improve our website.
  • Meet applicable legal or regulatory obligations.

4. Contact and Communication

If you contact us through WhatsApp, email, or another communication channel, the information you provide may be processed to respond to your inquiry and maintain relevant business communications.

Communications handled by third-party platforms are also subject to those platforms' own privacy policies.

5. Third-Party Services

Our website may use third-party services for hosting, website administration, database management, communication, analytics, or other operational purposes.

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms. We aim to use appropriate providers and reasonable safeguards.

6. Cookies and Analytics

Our website or its third-party services may use cookies or similar technologies to support website functionality, security, or analytics.

Where applicable, visitors can manage cookie preferences through their browser settings. Additional consent may be requested where required by applicable law.

7. Information Sharing

We do not intend to sell personal information submitted through our website. Information may be shared with relevant service providers when necessary to operate our services, respond to inquiries, protect our rights, or comply with legal obligations.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or loss. However, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to handle inquiries, maintain business records, provide services, resolve disputes, or meet applicable legal requirements.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable laws, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of certain personal information, or to object to certain processing.

You may contact us to make a privacy-related request. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

11. External Links

Our website may contain links to external websites and services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites.

12. Children's Privacy

Our website and business services are intended for professional and business audiences, not for children. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, services, or legal obligations change. Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised date.
